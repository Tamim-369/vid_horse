## Global Style (apply to every image)
- Aspect ratios: generate variants per situation for 9:16 (1080×1920, Shorts), 16:9 (1920×1080, long-form), 1:1 (1080×1080, square), and 4:5 (1080×1350, portrait). Use {ASPECT}. Keep subject in the center third with generous negative space so every ratio crops safely.
- Mood: dark, cinematic, noir, psychological, unsettling but elegant. No gore, no explicit content.
- Base: near-black gradient background (deep charcoal to black), heavy vignette, subtle film grain, soft accent glow in upper third.
- Composition: single focal subject centered, lots of negative space, high contrast, shallow depth of field feel.
- Lighting: chiaroscuro, one dominant light source, colored accent rim light.
- Color system by category (accent on black):
  - face: crimson accent (#b43c50)
  - body: steel blue (#5a78b4)
  - shadow: cold gray (#78788c)
  - control: amber (#a0783c)
  - space: teal (#508ca0)
  - text: off-white (#c8c8c8)
  - surveillance: phosphor green (#3ca078)
  - abstract: violet (#7850c8)
  - weather: slate blue (#648cb4)
  - motion: magenta (#c85064)
- No text, no logos, no watermarks, no stock-photo look. Original, painterly/photographic hybrid.
- Consistency: same grain, vignette, and lighting recipe every time; vary only subject and accent color.
